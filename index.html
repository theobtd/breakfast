7 Uncompromised Pure Ingredients

Every ingredient serves a clean functional purpose for performance, satiety, and healthy digestion.

01

US Organic Rolled Oats

Upper Midwest Millers

Complex, slow-digesting carbs providing steady, long-lasting energy without glucose spikes.

02

Cold-Milled Golden Flax

Montana Agriculture

Packed with anti-inflammatory Plant Omega-3s (ALA) and fiber to create a smooth, thick porridge texture.

03

Sunflower Seed Kernels

North Dakota Farms

Clean crunch loaded with Vitamin E for muscle cell recovery and bioavailable magnesium.

04

US Organic Chia Seeds

Kentucky/Tennessee

Hydrophilic seeds that absorb milk overnight, binding the mix while delivering dense soluble fiber.

05

California Raisins

Fresno Central Valley

Unsulfured, naturally sun-dried sweetness providing quick glycogen replenishment post-workout.

Southwest Processors
